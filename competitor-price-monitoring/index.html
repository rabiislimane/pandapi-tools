Competitor price monitoring: get an alert when a competitor changes prices

Paste a competitor's Shopify or WooCommerce store. Every morning you get a short list of what changed: price drops and increases (old price, new price, % change), new products, removed products and out of stock or back in stock. Unchanged products cost nothing.

About $0.30 a month to watch 3 competitor stores of 100 products every day. Apify's free plan gives you $5 of credit every month, so this runs free.

What the daily alert tells you

  • Price drop or price increase: product, variant, old price, new price and the % change.
  • New product: the full product row (title, price, stock, link, image), the day it appears.
  • Removed product: a product that's no longer in the store.
  • Out of stock and back in stock, per variant (size, colour).
  • New variant: a new size or colour on an existing product.
  • A free summary per store: number of each change and the biggest price drops, so the email reads like "3 prices dropped, 1 product out of stock".

Set it up in 3 minutes, no code

  1. Create a free Apify account (1 minute). Click Set it up in 3 minutes. Apify opens with our ready-made task already attached. Click Continue with Google (or type your email). It's free: $5 of credit every month, no card.
  2. Fill in two things. The task opens with an example filled in. Replace the example store with your competitors' store links, one per line (up to 10). Then type the email address you signed up with in Email me the report at (Apify only sends to your own account's address).
  3. Click Save & Start. About a minute later the first report is in your inbox. It lists the products it saved as the starting point; from then on you only hear about changes: price drops, price increases, out of stock, back in stock, new and removed products.
  4. Make it run every morning. In Apify's left menu click Schedules, then Create new. Choose Daily and a time, click Add > Task and pick your task, then Save and Enable (new schedules start switched off). Apify's guide with pictures: Schedules.
  5. Optional: Slack. On your task's Integrations tab add Slack and use this message: {{resource.statusMessage}}. It posts the report's first line ("Allbirds: 3 prices dropped, 1 item out of stock"). Apify's guide: Slack integration.

Questions

Which stores can I watch?

Any public Shopify or WooCommerce store. Paste the store's home page link; the platform is detected for you. Most direct-to-consumer brands run on Shopify.

How much does it cost?

About $0.30 a month for 3 stores of 100 products checked daily. You pay $0.50 per 1,000 products on the first run and $1 per 1,000 changes after that. Apify's free plan includes $5 of credit every month.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. You open the ready-made task, paste store links, click Schedule and add the email integration. Everything is buttons.

Will I get an email when nothing changed?

The run still finishes and you can choose to be emailed only when it succeeds. A day with no changes returns an empty list and costs nothing.

Can I see the price history?

Yes. Each change row carries the product's price history (every price change the monitor has seen, up to 200) plus the lowest and highest price seen. Download any run as Excel or CSV to open it in Google Sheets.

Is this allowed?

The tool reads the public product catalog every Shopify and WooCommerce store publishes for browsers and search engines, at a polite rate, without logging in.
