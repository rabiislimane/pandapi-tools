SpyFu alternative: see every Google ad a competitor runs, and get told about new ones

SpyFu is a keyword research tool first: it shows which keywords a competitor bids on, estimated ad spend and years of search ad history, from its own crawl of Google results. If what you really want is simpler, "what ads is this competitor running right now, and tell me when they launch a new one", Google already publishes that in its Ads Transparency Center. A weekly alert on top of it costs a few cents a month.

Where SpyFu is the better choice

  • You want the keywords a competitor bids on and how much they likely spend.
  • You do SEO research too: rankings, backlinks, keyword ideas.
  • You want years of search ad history in one dashboard.

The free way, for one competitor once: open Google's Ads Transparency Center, type the company's name and scroll. It has no alerts or export, which is what the tool above adds.

For developers: the same tracker is an API on Apify (Competitor Google Ads Tracker), with webhooks, n8n and Make integrations and an MCP server for AI agents.

Questions

Is there a free alternative to SpyFu?

For seeing a competitor's ads, yes: Google's Ads Transparency Center is free to browse. For a weekly email with only new ads, a scheduled tracker costs a few cents a month inside Apify's free $5 monthly credit. For keyword and spend data there is no free equivalent.

How much does SpyFu cost?

Third-party price trackers list Basic at $39 a month ($29 a month billed yearly) and Pro + AI at $119 a month ($89 billed yearly), checked 28 November 2026. SpyFu's own page has the current prices.

Can I see which keywords a competitor bids on?

Not with this tool. Google's Transparency Center doesn't publish keywords, so you need a keyword tool like SpyFu for that.

Which ads does it find?

Every ad Google lists for that advertiser in the Ads Transparency Center: text, image and video ads on Search, YouTube, Shopping, Maps and Play, with the first and last date each was shown.
