Export App Store reviews to CSV or Excel, for any app

App Store Connect lets you read your own app's reviews but has no CSV button, and it doesn't cover competitors at all. Here are the free ways to export App Store reviews, where they stop, and how to get reviews from every country into one file.

Updated 21 November 2026 · by the Pandapi team

Free way 1: App Store Connect (your own apps)

You can read and reply to reviews in App Store Connect, and the App Store Connect API has a customerReviews endpoint. It needs an API key and covers only apps in your developer account.

Free way 2: Apple's public reviews feed (any app)

Apple serves each app's recent reviews as a public feed, 50 per page and 10 pages at most:

https://itunes.apple.com/us/rss/customerreviews/page=1/id=570060128/sortby=mostrecent/json

Loop pages 1 to 10, read feed.entry, and write date, stars, version, title and text to CSV. It's free and works for any public app. The catch: the feed stops at 500 reviews per country and sort order, each of about 175 storefronts is a separate feed, and running it again gives you the same reviews again.

The API way: many apps, every country, only new reviews

  1. Open the App Store Reviews Scraper on Apify.
  2. Paste App Store links, app IDs, bundle IDs or app names, and pick countries (or all of them).
  3. Filter by stars (e.g. 1 to 2 for complaints), date and keywords before you pay for anything.
  4. Turn on onlyNewReviews and schedule it weekly: later runs return only reviews written since the last run.
curl -X POST "https://api.apify.com/v2/acts/blueberry_rainstorm~app-store-reviews-scraper/run-sync-get-dataset-items?token=$APIFY_TOKEN" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{
  "apps": [
    "https://apps.apple.com/us/app/duolingo-language-lessons/id570060128"
  ],
  "countries": [
    "us",
    "gb",
    "ca",
    "au"
  ],
  "maxReviewsPerApp": 1000,
  "minRating": 1,
  "maxRating": 2
}'
# pip install apify-client
from apify_client import ApifyClient

client = ApifyClient("YOUR_APIFY_TOKEN")
run = client.actor("blueberry_rainstorm/app-store-reviews-scraper").call(run_input={
    "apps": [
        "notion",
        "evernote"
    ],
    "countries": [
        "us"
    ],
    "maxReviewsPerApp": 500,
    "includeSummary": True
})
for item in client.dataset(run["defaultDatasetId"]).iterate_items():
    if item.get("type") == "review":
        print(item["rating"], item["date"], item["title"])

Each run also writes a free insights summary per app: rating by version and country, top complaint and praise phrases, and the worst recent reviews. Reviewer names are not stored.

Questions

Can I export App Store reviews for free?

Yes. Apple's public RSS feed gives the latest 500 reviews per country and sort order for any app, and App Store Connect's API covers your own apps. For more countries, filters and monitoring, the API costs $0.15 per 1,000 reviews and Apify's free credit covers over 30,000 a month.

Why can't I get more than 500 reviews?

Apple's feed caps each country and sort order at 500. The scraper combines most-recent and most-helpful orders (up to about 1,000 unique per country) and lets you add every storefront.

Can I get competitor reviews?

Yes. Reviews are public, so any app on the App Store works: paste its link, ID or name.

Does it include reviewer names?

No. We leave reviewer names out on purpose. You get the rating, title, text, date, app version and country.
