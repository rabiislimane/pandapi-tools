Website technology lookup: see what any list of websites is built with

Paste a list of websites, your prospects or competitors. You get one row per site with its CMS, ecommerce platform, analytics, ad pixels, payments, hosting and email provider, from 7,700 technologies, plus its social links. Put it on a weekly schedule and you hear when a prospect switches platform or adds a tool.

$1.50 per 1,000 websites; a weekly watch on 500 prospects where 20 change is about $0.13 a month after the first run. Apify's free plan gives you $5 of credit every month.

What each run tells you

  • CMS and ecommerce (WordPress, Shopify, Webflow, WooCommerce...), analytics and ad pixels, payments, live chat, CRM and marketing tools.
  • Hosting, CDN and email provider from the site and its DNS records.
  • Social links (LinkedIn, Facebook, Instagram, X, YouTube, TikTok) in the same row.
  • With monitoring on: only sites that changed, with the technologies added and removed.
  • Evidence for each detection, so you can trust a lead list before you send it.

Set it up in 3 minutes, no code

  1. Create a free Apify account (1 minute). Click Set it up in 3 minutes. Apify opens with our ready-made task attached. Click Continue with Google (or type your email). It's free: $5 of credit every month, no card.
  2. Fill in two things. Paste your list of websites, one per line (a domain like allbirds.com is enough). Then type the email address you signed up with in Email me the report at (Apify only sends to your own account's address).
  3. Click Save & Start. About a minute later the first report is in your inbox: one line per site with its platform, analytics, ad pixels, email tools, chat and payments. Export the full table to Excel or CSV from the run's Overview tab.
  4. Make it run every week. In Apify's left menu click Schedules, then Create new. Choose Weekly and a time, click Add > Task and pick your task, then Save and Enable (new schedules start switched off). Apify's guide with pictures: Schedules. Later runs return only sites that added or removed a technology; quiet weeks send no email.
  5. Optional: Slack. On your task's Integrations tab add Slack and use this message: {{resource.statusMessage}}. It posts the report's first line ("2 of 50 websites changed their technology: allbirds.com, rothys.com"). Apify's guide: Slack integration.

Questions

How does it know what a site is built with?

It reads the site's home page, scripts, headers and cookies and its DNS records, and matches them against 7,700 known technologies.

How much does it cost?

$1.50 per 1,000 sites where something is found; unreachable sites are free. A 2,000-site list costs $3, and a weekly watch on 500 prospects is about $0.13 a month.

Can it find all websites using Shopify?

No. You bring the list of websites (from your CRM, a directory or a search) and it tells you what each one runs.

Can I see when a prospect changes platform?

Yes. Turn on monitoring and schedule it weekly: later runs return only the sites that changed, with what was added and removed.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. You open the ready-made task, paste websites, type your email and click Start. Add a weekly schedule to hear when a site changes.
