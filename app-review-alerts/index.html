App Store review monitoring: get every new review of any app, every morning

Paste your app, or a competitor's, from the App Store or Google Play. Every morning you get only the reviews written since yesterday: stars, title, text, country and app version, plus a free summary (average rating, share of 1 and 2 star reviews, the words people complain about). Catch a bad update the day it ships.

About $0.10 a month to watch 5 apps in 3 countries that get 20 new reviews a day. Apify's free plan gives you $5 of credit every month, so this runs free.

What the daily alert tells you

  • Only new reviews since the last run, remembered per alert name, so nothing arrives twice.
  • Stars, title, text, date, country and app version for each review; on Google Play also the developer's reply.
  • A free summary: average rating, rating split, share of negative reviews, top complaint words and ratings by app version.
  • Filters: only 1 and 2 star reviews, keywords ("crash", "refund"), several countries or languages at once.
  • Works for competitors' apps too: see what their users hate before you build the same thing.

Set it up in 3 minutes, no code

  1. Open the ready-made task. Click Set it up in 3 minutes (or the Google Play version), create a free Apify account (no card), replace the example app with your app's store link, pick countries, and click Save & Start. The first run saves today's reviews as the starting point.
  2. Make it run every morning. In Apify's left menu click Schedules, then Create new. Choose Daily, click Add > Task and pick your task, then Save and Enable. Apify's guide: Schedules.
  3. Get it in Slack or Google Drive. On the task, open Integrations and add Slack or Google Drive, so new reviews land in your team channel or a spreadsheet.

Questions

Which apps can I watch?

Any public app on the App Store or Google Play: yours or a competitor's. Paste the store link; add several apps, one per line.

How much does it cost?

$0.15 per 1,000 App Store reviews and $0.10 per 1,000 Google Play reviews. 5 apps in 3 countries with 20 new reviews a day is about $0.10 a month, inside Apify's free $5 monthly credit.

Will I get the same review twice?

No. The alert remembers the reviews it returned under your alert name and only returns new ones next time.

Can I get only bad reviews?

Yes. Set the maximum rating to 2 to get only 1 and 2 star reviews, or add keywords like "crash" or "refund".

Do you collect reviewer names?

No. Rows have the review, rating, date, country and version, not the reviewer's name.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. You open the ready-made task, paste your app's link, click Schedule and add Slack or Google Drive.
