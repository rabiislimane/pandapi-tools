See your competitors' Google ads, and get told when they launch new ones

Type a competitor's website. You get every ad they run on Google (Search, YouTube, Shopping, Maps and Play) from Google's own Ads Transparency Center, with the ad copy, format and first and last shown dates. Put it on a weekly schedule and you get only the ads that started or stopped, so you see a new campaign the week it launches.

About $0.40 for the first look at 3 competitors, then a few cents a month for weekly new-ad alerts. Apify's free plan gives you $5 of credit every month, so this runs free.

What the weekly alert tells you

  • New ads a competitor started since last week, with headline, description, display URL and sitelinks where Google keeps them as text.
  • Stopped ads: ads that were running last week and aren't now.
  • Format and where it runs: text, image or video, with a link to the ad on Google's Transparency Center.
  • First and last shown dates and days running, so you can tell a test from a long-running winner.
  • A free summary per competitor: ads running now, started in the last 30 days, longest running.

Set it up in 3 minutes, no code

  1. Create a free Apify account (1 minute). Click Set it up in 3 minutes. Apify opens with our ready-made task attached. Click Continue with Google (or type your email). It's free: $5 of credit every month, no card.
  2. Fill in two things. Replace allbirds.com with your competitors' websites or names, one per line. Then type the email address you signed up with in Email me the report at (Apify only sends to your own account's address).
  3. Click Save & Start. About a minute later the first report is in your inbox: every ad your competitors run now, saved as the starting point. From then on you only hear about new ads.
  4. Make it run every week. In Apify's left menu click Schedules, then Create new. Choose Weekly (or Daily), click Add > Task and pick your task, then Save and Enable (new schedules start switched off). Apify's guide with pictures: Schedules. Weeks with no new ads send no email.
  5. Optional: Slack. On your task's Integrations tab add Slack and use this message: {{resource.statusMessage}}. It posts the report's first line ("3 new ads from your competitors (allbirds.com 2, rothys.com 1)"). Apify's guide: Slack integration.

Questions

Where do the ads come from?

From Google's public Ads Transparency Center, which lists every ad an advertiser has shown on Google Search, YouTube, Shopping, Maps and Play in the past.

How much does it cost?

$1.20 per 1,000 ads. A first look at 3 competitors with 100 ads each is about $0.36, and a weekly alert after that is a few cents a month. Apify's free plan includes $5 of credit every month.

Does it show the ad text?

Yes, for text ads: headline, description, display URL and sitelinks where Google keeps them as text, or read from the ad image. Image and video ads come with a preview link.

Can I see ads in one country only?

Yes. Set Region to a country (for example United Kingdom) to see only ads shown there.

Does it show keywords or ad spend?

No. Google's Transparency Center doesn't publish keywords or spend, so no tool built on it can show them.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. You open the ready-made task, type competitor websites and your email, and add a weekly schedule.
