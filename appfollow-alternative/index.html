Questions

Is there a cheaper alternative to AppFollow?

If you need review data rather than a reply inbox, yes: pay-per-review APIs cost $0.10 to $0.15 per 1,000 reviews, and Apify's free credit covers tens of thousands a month.

Can I export competitor app reviews?

Yes. App Store and Google Play reviews are public; paste the app link or name.

Does it tag sentiment?

The free summary lists top complaint and praise phrases and the worst recent reviews. For full sentiment tagging, send the CSV to your own model or a suite.
