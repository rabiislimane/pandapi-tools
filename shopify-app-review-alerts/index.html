Shopify App Store reviews: get every new review of your app and your competitors', daily

Paste your Shopify app and the apps you compete with. Every morning you get only the reviews posted since yesterday: stars, text, the merchant's country, how long they had used the app and the developer's reply. A free summary per app adds the reply rate and a suspicious-review check (bursts of short 5-star reviews from stores that installed the app the same day). This is about merchant reviews on apps.shopify.com, not Shopify's app approval review.

About $0.05 a month to watch your app and 4 competitors that get a few new reviews a day. No start fee. Apify's free plan gives you $5 of credit every month, so this runs free.

What the daily alert tells you

  • Only new reviews since the last run, remembered under your alert name, so nothing arrives twice.
  • Stars, text, date, country, how long the merchant had used the app, and the developer's reply with its date.
  • A free summary per app: average rating next to the listing's, reply rate and median reply time, ratings by country and by how long merchants used the app, top complaint words.
  • A free suspicious-review check: review bursts, 5-star reviews after less than a day of use, repeated wording, very short 5-star reviews, as a 0 to 100 score with plain reasons.
  • Filters: only 1 and 2 star reviews (a competitor's unhappy merchants are your best leads), a start date, or keywords like "billing" or "support".

Set it up in 3 minutes, no code

  1. Create a free Apify account (1 minute). Click Set it up in 3 minutes. Apify opens with our ready-made task attached. Click Continue with Google (or type your email). It's free: $5 of credit every month, no card.
  2. Fill in two things. Replace the two example apps with your app and your competitors', one link per line (for example https://apps.shopify.com/judgeme). Then type the email address you signed up with in Email me the report at (Apify only sends to your own account's address). Want complaints only? Set Maximum stars to 2.
  3. Click Save & Start. About a minute later the first report is in your inbox: the latest reviews, saved as the starting point. From then on you only hear about new reviews, worst rating first, with the merchant's country.
  4. Make it run every morning. In Apify's left menu click Schedules, then Create new. Choose Daily and a time, click Add > Task and pick your task, then Save and Enable (new schedules start switched off). Apify's guide with pictures: Schedules. Days with no new reviews send no email.
  5. Optional: Slack. On your task's Integrations tab add Slack and use this message: {{resource.statusMessage}}. It posts the report's first line ("6 new reviews (Judge.me 4, Loox 2), average 3.2 stars, 2 of them 1-2 stars"). Apify's guide: Slack integration.

Questions

Which apps can I watch?

Any public app on the Shopify App Store: yours or a competitor's. Paste the app's link or its handle, one per line.

How much does it cost?

$0.10 per 1,000 reviews, no start fee. Your app and 4 competitors with a few new reviews a day is about $0.05 a month, inside Apify's free $5 monthly credit.

Will I get the same review twice?

No. The alert remembers the reviews it returned under your alert name and returns only new ones next time.

Can it tell if a competitor's reviews are fake?

It flags patterns worth a look, with reasons: bursts of reviews on one day, 5-star reviews from stores that had the app for less than a day, repeated wording. These are signals, not proof: some apps honestly ask for a review right after install.

Is this about Shopify's app approval review?

No. This watches the reviews merchants leave on your app's App Store listing, not Shopify's review of your app submission.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. You open the ready-made task, paste app links, click Start and add a daily schedule.
