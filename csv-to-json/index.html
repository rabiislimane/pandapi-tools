      How to use it

      1. Paste CSV, or copy cells in Excel or Google Sheets and paste them (they arrive tab-separated, which is detected). You can also drop a .csv, .tsv or .xlsx file; for a workbook, pick one sheet or all sheets.
      2. Check the options: the separator is found for you, numbers and true/false become real JSON values, and anything with a leading zero or more than 15 digits stays text so zip codes and IDs survive. Turn on Nested keys to make customer.name a nested object.
      3. Pick the shape: an array of objects (most APIs), JSON Lines (one record per line, for BigQuery, MongoDB or AI fine-tuning files), an object keyed by a column, or arrays. Copy the JSON or download it.
      4. To convert a folder of files, drop them all at once and download one .zip.

      Questions

      Are my files uploaded?

      No. The CSV and Excel files are read in your browser, so customer lists, exports and reports never leave your computer, and the page works offline once loaded.

      Why do my Excel dates come out as dates?

      Excel stores a date as a day number (46086 is 5 March 2026) and only its cell format says it is a date. This tool reads the cell formats, so dates come out as 2026-03-05, date-times as 2026-03-06T14:30:00 and times as 09:15:00. Numbers come out as Excel shows them, so 0.1+0.2 is 0.3, not 0.30000000000000004.

      Which values become numbers?

      Plain numbers like 42, -3.5 or 1e3. Values with a leading zero (00721), more than 15 digits, a plus sign or spaces stay text, because turning them into numbers would change them. Choose "everything as text" to keep every value as a string.

      My CSV shows strange characters like Ã©. What now?

      Files are read as UTF-8 when they are valid UTF-8, and as Windows-1252 (what older Excel saves) when they are not, so accents like é and ü come out right without picking an encoding.

      Can it read .xls or .ods files?

      Not yet. Save old .xls (Excel 97-2003) and .ods files as .xlsx or .csv first. Password-protected workbooks can't be read either.
