Privacy policy

Last updated 25 November 2026

The short version. The free tools on this site run in your browser, so your files and text never leave your device. We use no cookies, no analytics and no ads. Our paid APIs run inside your own Apify account, and Apify does not give us your inputs or results. We don't sell or share personal data, and we don't send marketing emails.

Who we are

Pandapi is a small independent developer brand. We make the free tools on this site and a set of paid data APIs published on Apify under apify.com/blueberry_rainstorm. In this policy "we" and "us" means Pandapi. See Contact and about for how to reach us.

The free tools on this website

  • Every tool (HTML to Markdown, PDF to Markdown, JSON to Excel and the rest) does its work with code running in your browser. The text you paste and the files you drop are processed on your device. They are never uploaded to us or to anyone else.
  • We don't ask for an account, a name or an email address.
  • The pages load no analytics, no ads, no tracking pixels and no third-party scripts. The site's security rules block pages from sending data to other servers.
  • We don't set cookies or save anything in your browser's storage. See the cookie notice.

What our web host sees

This site is a set of static files hosted by GitHub Pages. Like every web host, GitHub receives your IP address and the page you asked for, and may keep it in its logs for security. GitHub does not share these logs with us. GitHub's own privacy statement covers that data.

We use Google Search Console, which shows us totals such as how many people found a page through Google search and which search words they used. It doesn't tell us who you are.

Links to other sites

Links from this site to our APIs on Apify carry a short tag (for example utm_source=pandapi-tools) so that Apify's statistics can show which page sent a visitor. Once you are on another site, such as Apify, GitHub or X, that site's privacy policy applies.

Our paid APIs on Apify

  • You run our APIs in your own Apify account. Apify handles your account, payment and the storage of your runs and results, under the Apify privacy policy. We never see your card or bank details.
  • Apify does not give us your inputs, results or logs. We see general numbers Apify shows to every API developer, such as how many runs and paid results our APIs had.
  • There are two exceptions, both in your hands. If you turn on Share run data with developers in your Apify settings, or put a run link in an issue you send us, Apify lets us open that run so we can help you.
  • Some APIs listed on RapidAPI work the same way: RapidAPI handles your account and keys, and requests pass through to Apify.

Personal data in the results you collect

Some of our APIs return public information that can include personal data, for example business email addresses found on a website, or reviewer names in app reviews. When you collect that data, you decide what to do with it, so you are responsible for using it lawfully. That includes data protection laws such as the GDPR and anti-spam laws such as CAN-SPAM. Please read our terms before collecting personal data.

When you contact us

If you write to us through an Apify issue, a GitHub issue or a message on X, we use what you send only to answer you and to fix the problem. GitHub issues are public, so don't put private details in them. We keep messages only as long as needed to help you, and we don't add you to any list.

Report emails

Two of our APIs can email you a short report after each run. Those emails are sent by Apify, inside your account, only to your own Apify account address. We don't receive or store your email address. See Report emails for how they work and how to stop them.

Children

This site and our APIs are made for adults at work. They are not aimed at children under 16.

Your choices and rights

We hold almost no personal data about you. If you want to know what we have, or want it deleted, contact us and we'll answer. For data in your Apify account (your profile, runs, results and invoices), Apify is the right place to ask, and you can delete runs and results yourself in Apify Console.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle data, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
