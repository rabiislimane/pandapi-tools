Prisync alternative: competitor price alerts for Shopify stores

Prisync is a solid price-tracking tool for retailers: you list your products, add each competitor's product URL, and it checks prices up to 3 times a day, from $99 a month. Many small Shopify brands only want one thing: an email when a competitor changes a price, launches a product or runs out of stock. That costs a few cents a month.

Updated 25 November 2026 · by the Pandapi team · We make one of the tools compared here, so we list where the others are better too.

Competitor Price Monitor: watching 3 competitor stores of 100 products every day costs about $0.30 a month. Unchanged products are free, and Apify's free plan gives you $5 of credit every month.

Try Competitor Price Monitor

The short answer

  • Your competitors sell on Shopify or WooCommerce (most direct-to-consumer brands do): paste their store links and get a daily change list for about $0.30 a month, with no product mapping. See how it works.
  • You need Amazon, Walmart or big retailers, repricing rules or a team dashboard: stay with Prisync or a similar tool. Ours can't do those.

Set it up in 3 minutes, no code

  1. Open the ready-made task and create a free Apify account (Google sign-in works).
  2. Replace the example with your competitors' store links, one per line, and type your email in Email me the report at. Click Save & Start.
  3. Click Schedules, then Create new, then Daily, and add your task. Every morning you get the changes by email.

Where Prisync is the better choice

  • You compete with Amazon, Walmart or retailers that don't run on Shopify or WooCommerce.
  • You want automatic repricing rules or MAP monitoring.
  • A team needs one dashboard with your products matched to competitors'.

For developers: the same monitor is an API on Apify (Competitor Price Monitor), with webhooks, n8n and Make integrations and an MCP server for AI agents.

Questions

Is there a cheaper alternative to Prisync?

If your competitors run on Shopify or WooCommerce, yes: a scheduled price monitor on Apify costs about $0.30 a month for 3 stores of 100 products checked daily, and Apify's free plan includes $5 of credit a month.

How much does Prisync cost?

Its URL-based plans are listed at $99 a month for 100 products, $199 for 1,000 and $399 for 5,000, with higher prices for its other plans (checked 25 November 2026).

Can I track competitor prices for free?

Yes, within Apify's free $5 monthly credit: you pay $0.50 per 1,000 products on the first run and $1 per 1,000 changes after that, and unchanged products are free.

Does it work on Amazon?

No. It reads the public catalog of Shopify and WooCommerce stores. For Amazon and big retailers, Prisync or a similar tool is the better pick.
