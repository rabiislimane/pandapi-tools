Find business emails, phones and socials from a list of websites

You have a list of company websites and need their public contact details in your CRM. Checking each Contact page by hand takes about two minutes a site. Here's the free way for a few, and how to do 1,000 in one run.

The free way

Open the site, look at the footer, the Contact and About pages, and copy the email and phone. Browser extensions that pull emails from the open page help a little. Our free link extractor lists every mailto: and tel: link on a page you paste, which is quick for a handful of sites.

The API way: one row per website

  1. Open the Website Contact Finder on Apify.
  2. Paste your list of websites (or upload a CSV in the console).
  3. Keep genericEmailsOnly on to return role addresses like info@ and sales@ rather than people's personal addresses.
  4. Run it and export to CSV, Excel, Google Sheets, HubSpot (via Make or n8n) or JSON.
curl -X POST "https://api.apify.com/v2/acts/blueberry_rainstorm~website-contact-finder/run-sync-get-dataset-items?token=$APIFY_TOKEN" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{
  "startUrls": [
    {
      "url": "https://apify.com"
    },
    {
      "url": "https://crawlee.dev"
    }
  ],
  "maxPagesPerSite": 8,
  "genericEmailsOnly": true
}'

The crawler reads the home page first, then the pages most likely to hold contacts (contact, about, imprint, team, support), up to maxPagesPerSite. You get one row per site: emails, phones, LinkedIn, Facebook, Instagram, X, YouTube, TikTok and the postal address when the site shows it, with the page each item came from.

What it costs

$5 per 1,000 websites where contacts were found. Sites with nothing found, or that block the crawler, are free. A list of 400 sites where 300 have contacts costs $1.50.

Use it responsibly

  • It reads only public pages and respects robots.txt by default.
  • It's built for enriching companies you already work with or research, not for bulk cold email. Follow anti-spam and data protection law (GDPR, CAN-SPAM, CASL) where you use the data.
  • It doesn't guess or verify personal addresses; for that kind of tool see Hunter.io alternatives.

Questions

How do I find the email address of a website?

Check the footer, Contact, About and Imprint pages. To do it for many sites, the Website Contact Finder reads those pages for you and returns one row per site.

Do I pay for sites with no email?

No. You pay $5 per 1,000 websites where contacts were found; sites with nothing found or that block the crawler are free.

Does it find personal emails?

By default it returns role and business addresses (info@, sales@, support@) published on the site. It doesn't guess or pattern-match personal addresses.

Can I export to my CRM?

Yes. Download CSV or Excel, or send rows to HubSpot, Pipedrive or Google Sheets with Apify, Make or n8n integrations.
