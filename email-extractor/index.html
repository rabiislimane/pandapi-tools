    How to use it

    1. Paste text from anywhere: an email thread, a web page, a spreadsheet, a CRM export or a document. Or drop files, several at once: PDF, Word (.docx), Excel (.xlsx), CSV, saved emails (.eml), contact cards (.vcf) and web pages (.html).
    2. Check the list. Each address appears once, with the name written next to it, how often it was seen and where (file, page, sheet and row). Untick role addresses, free mailboxes or no-replies to drop them, or search for one company's domain.
    3. Download Excel (an Emails sheet and a Domains sheet) or CSV, or copy the addresses one per line, comma separated, or semicolon separated to paste straight into Outlook's To box.

    Questions

    Is my data uploaded?

    No. Everything runs in your browser, so contracts, inboxes, CRM exports and customer lists never leave your computer. You can turn off your internet after the page loads and it still works.

    Which files can it read?

    PDF (with a text layer, not scans), Word .docx, Excel .xlsx, CSV and TSV, saved emails (.eml and .mbox), vCard contact files (.vcf), calendar files (.ics), web pages (.html) and any plain text file. Old .doc and .xls files must be saved as .docx or .xlsx first.

    How does it find the name?

    It takes the name written right before the address, as in Maria Ortega <maria@...> or "Ortega, Maria" <...>, or right after it in brackets, and the visible text of mailto links. If no name is next to an address, the Name column stays empty rather than guessing.

    What do the types mean?

    Role is a shared inbox like info@, sales@ or support@. Free mailbox is a personal account at Gmail, Outlook, Yahoo and about 100 other providers. No-reply addresses can't receive mail. Everything else is marked personal.

    Does it check that the addresses work?

    No. It finds and cleans addresses that are written correctly; it does not send mail or contact any server. Image names like logo@2x.png, template addresses such as name@example.com and tracking ids are left out.

    Can I paste a website address?

    Not here: a page running in your browser can't visit other websites. Open the page, select all and copy, or save it and drop the .html file. To get the emails of many websites at once, use the Website Contact Finder linked on this page.
