Hunter.io alternatives for finding company contact details

Hunter is built to find a specific person's work email and verify it. A lot of people searching for "Hunter alternative" actually need something simpler: the published contact details of a list of companies for a CRM. That's a different tool, and much cheaper.

Updated 21 November 2026 · by the Pandapi team · We make one of the tools compared here, so we list where the others are better too.

Website Contact Finder: $5 per 1,000 websites with contacts (no contacts = free). Apify's free plan gives you $5 of credit every month, which covers about 1,000 websites a month. No start fee; failed items are free.

If you need named decision-makers and verified addresses, Hunter (or Apollo, Snov.io) is the right tool. If you need "how do I reach this company", the site already tells you, and reading it is cheap.

Example: 500 suppliers into a CRM

  1. Export the websites to a list.
  2. Paste them into the Website Contact Finder.
  3. Download the CSV: emails, phones, LinkedIn, Facebook, Instagram, X and address per site.
  4. Import into HubSpot, Pipedrive or a sheet.

If 400 of the 500 publish contacts, that costs $2.

Use it lawfully

Use published business contacts for relevant B2B outreach and follow GDPR, CAN-SPAM and CASL. Role addresses (info@, sales@) are the default; personal addresses are off unless you change it.

Questions

What is a cheaper alternative to Hunter.io?

For company-level contacts (role emails, phones, socials), a website crawler like Pandapi's Website Contact Finder at $5 per 1,000 sites with results. For verified personal emails, Hunter's free plan (50 credits a month) or similar tools.

Does Hunter.io have a free plan?

Yes, 50 credits a month per its pricing page; paid plans start at $49 a month.

Does the contact finder verify emails?

No. It returns addresses the site itself publishes, with the page they were found on.
