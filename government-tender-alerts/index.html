What the daily alert tells you

  • Only new notices: alert mode remembers every notice it sent you under your alert name.
  • Title, buyer, country and portal, with a link to the official notice.
  • Deadline and days left; tenders that already closed are hidden.
  • Value in the original currency and converted to US dollars and euros, where the buyer published it.
  • Filters you can add: exclude words, countries, CPV or NAICS codes, minimum value, tenders only or pre-notices too.

Set it up in 3 minutes, no code

  1. Create a free Apify account (1 minute). Click Set it up in 3 minutes. Apify opens with our ready-made task already attached. Click Continue with Google (or type your email). It's free: $5 of credit every month, no card.
  2. Fill in two things. The task opens with an example filled in. Put your own words in Keywords (for example "cleaning services" or "cybersecurity") and give the alert a name. Then type the email address you signed up with in Email me the report at (Apify only sends to your own account's address).
  3. Click Save & Start. About a minute later the first report is in your inbox. It lists the matching tenders, soonest deadline first, with buyer, value and link; from then on you only get tenders you haven't seen.
  4. Make it run every morning. In Apify's left menu click Schedules, then Create new. Choose Daily and a time, click Add > Task and pick your task, then Save and Enable (new schedules start switched off). Apify's guide with pictures: Schedules.
  5. Optional: Slack. On your task's Integrations tab add Slack and use this message: {{resource.statusMessage}}. It posts the report's first line ("4 new tenders on your keywords (US 3, GB 1)"). Apify's guide: Slack integration.

Questions

Which portals are covered?

SAM.gov (US federal), TED (EU and EEA), Contracts Finder and Find a Tender (UK), CanadaBuys and World Bank procurement. All official, public sources.

How much does it cost?

$2 per 1,000 notices returned and nothing for notices filtered out. A daily alert with about 10 new tenders costs about $0.60 a month, inside Apify's free $5 monthly credit.

Will I get the same tender twice?

No. Alert mode remembers what it sent under your alert name and returns only new notices on the next run.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. You open the ready-made task, type your keywords and your email, and add a daily schedule.

Can I filter by country or category code?

Yes: countries (ISO codes like US, GB, DE), CPV codes for Europe, NAICS codes for the US, a minimum value, and words to exclude.

Does it include US state and local tenders?

Not yet. In the US it covers federal notices from SAM.gov.
