Price2Spy alternative: daily competitor price alerts for Shopify stores

Price2Spy is a mature price-monitoring tool for retailers and brands: it matches your products to competitors' pages on almost any site, including marketplaces, and adds repricing and MAP monitoring. Since spring 2026 it asks you to start a trial or contact sales instead of listing prices. If your competitors run on Shopify or WooCommerce and you just want an email when they cut prices, launch products or sell out, that costs a few cents a month.

Updated 27 November 2026 · by the Pandapi team · We make one of the tools compared here, so we list where the others are better too.

The short answer

  • Your competitors sell on Shopify or WooCommerce: paste their store links and get a daily list of what changed for about $0.30 a month, with no product matching. See how it works.
  • You compete on Amazon or marketplaces, or you need repricing, MAP checks or several price checks a day: Price2Spy (or Prisync) is the better tool.

Set it up in 3 minutes, no code

  1. Open the ready-made task and create a free Apify account (Google sign-in works).
  2. Replace the example with your competitors' store links, one per line, and type your email in Email me the report at. Click Save & Start.
  3. Click Schedules, then Create new, then Daily, and add your task. Every morning you get only what changed.

Where Price2Spy is the better choice

  • Your competitors sell on Amazon, eBay or sites that don't run on Shopify or WooCommerce.
  • You want repricing rules, MAP monitoring or up to 8 price checks a day.
  • A team needs a dashboard with your products matched to competitors' and an account manager.

Questions

Is there a cheaper alternative to Price2Spy?

If your competitors run on Shopify or WooCommerce, yes: a scheduled price monitor on Apify costs about $0.30 a month for 3 stores of 100 products checked daily, inside Apify's free $5 monthly credit.

How much does Price2Spy cost?

Price2Spy stopped listing plan prices publicly in spring 2026; you start a free trial or ask for a quote. Before that, its Starter plan was listed at $39.95 a month and Basic at $157.95 (checked 27 November 2026).

Do I have to match my products to competitors' products?

No. You paste a competitor's store link and every product in its catalog is watched. The daily alert lists price changes, new and removed products and stock changes.

Does it work on Amazon?

No. It reads the public catalog of Shopify and WooCommerce stores. For Amazon and marketplaces, Price2Spy or Prisync is the better pick.
