About Pandapi

Pandapi is a small independent developer brand. We build two things:

  • Free tools that run in your browser, on this site: converters for HTML, PDF, Word, Markdown, JSON, CSV and Excel, plus link and email extractors. No sign-up and no upload.
  • Data APIs and alerts on Apify, at apify.com/blueberry_rainstorm: competitor price alerts, government tender alerts, app review exports, website contact finder and more. You pay per result through Apify.

We study the best-known tools for each job before building ours, and we publish honest comparisons in our guides, including when another tool is the better choice.

How to reach us

  • A problem with one of our APIs: open an issue on the API's Issues tab on Apify. Add the run link so we can see what happened. This is the fastest way.
  • Questions, ideas or a free sample report: open an issue at github.com/rabroubx-coder/pandapi-examples. GitHub issues are public, so don't include private details.
  • A private message: send a direct message to @PandapiDev on X.

We read messages every day and aim to reply within 2 working days.
