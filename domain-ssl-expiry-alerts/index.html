What the weekly report tells you

  • Domain expiry date and days left, registrar and registry locks, straight from the registry (1,200+ extensions).
  • SSL certificate: issuer, expiry date, days left, and whether it covers the host and is trusted.
  • Email security: SPF with its DNS-lookup count (over 10 and mail fails), DMARC policy, DKIM keys, MTA-STS.
  • A plain problem list per domain: domain expires within 30 days, certificate expires within 14 days, no DMARC, too many SPF lookups.
  • Optional change mode (Only changes since my last run): later runs return only domains where something changed, and unchanged domains cost nothing. A domain or certificate entering its warning window counts as a change, so you hear about it once when it gets within 30 days, again in its last week, and on the day it expires, not every week. Renewals, new certificates, new mail providers and DMARC changes show up too.

Set it up in 3 minutes, no code

  1. Create a free Apify account (1 minute). Click Set it up in 3 minutes. Apify opens with our ready-made task attached. Click Continue with Google (or type your email). It's free: $5 of credit every month, no card.
  2. Fill in two things. Replace the example domains with yours, one per line (links and email addresses work too). Then type the email address you signed up with in Email me the report at (Apify only sends to your own account's address).
  3. Click Save & Start. About a minute later you have the first report: every domain and certificate that expires soon, soonest first. If one needs attention, it's in your inbox too.
  4. Make it run every week. In Apify's left menu click Schedules, then Create new. Choose Weekly, click Add > Task and pick your task, then Save and Enable (new schedules start switched off). Apify's guide with pictures: Schedules. Quiet weeks send no email.
  5. Optional: Slack. On your task's Integrations tab add Slack and use this message: {{resource.statusMessage}}. It posts the report's first line ("1 domain needs attention: 1 domain expires within 30 days"). Apify's guide: Slack integration.

Questions

Where do the expiry dates come from?

Domain dates come from each registry's official RDAP service (WHOIS where a registry has none). SSL dates come from the certificate each site serves right now.

How much does it cost?

$1.50 per 1,000 domains checked. A weekly report on 50 domains is about $0.30 a month, inside Apify's free $5 monthly credit. Domains the registry didn't answer for are free.

Will it email me 30 days before expiry?

Yes. Type your Apify account email in "Email me the report at". The weekly email comes when a domain expires within 30 days or a certificate within 14 days (you can change both numbers), with one line per domain and the expiry date. A week with nothing to report sends nothing.

Will it remind me every week about the same domain?

Not in change mode. Switch on "Only changes since my last run" and a domain is reported once when it enters the 30-day window, once in its last week and once when it expires. In the plain weekly report every domain is listed every week.

Some domains show no expiry date. Why?

A few registries (for example .de) don't publish expiry dates. The report says so for those domains.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. You open the ready-made task, paste your domains, type your email and add a weekly schedule.
