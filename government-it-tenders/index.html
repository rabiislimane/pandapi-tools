How this list is made

  • Every Monday a free scheduled job reads the official open-data feeds of SAM.gov, TED, Find a Tender, Contracts Finder, CanadaBuys, World Bank. No login, no API key.
  • It keeps open tenders (not awards) first published in the last 7 days whose text matches software, cloud, IT services, cybersecurity, data centre, data center, and that have the word in the title or an IT classification code.
  • No personal data: contact names, emails and phone numbers in the notices are left out. Open each notice for the buyer's contact details.
  • The same code runs the Government Tenders API on Apify, where you choose your own keywords, countries, codes and minimum value.

Questions

Where do these tenders come from?

From the official open-data feeds of SAM.gov (US federal), TED (EU and EEA), Find a Tender and Contracts Finder (UK), CanadaBuys and World Bank procurement. Each title links to the official notice.

How often is the list updated?

Every Monday morning (UTC). It lists open tenders first published in the 7 days before, soonest deadline first.

Which tenders count as IT?

Notices whose text matches software, cloud, IT services, cybersecurity or data centre, and that also have the word in the title or an IT classification code (CPV 48, 72 or 302, IT NAICS or PSC codes, UNSPSC 43 or 81).

Can I get tenders for my own keywords every day?

Yes. The daily tender alert uses the same six sources with your own keywords, countries and minimum value, and sends only new notices by email or Slack. It costs about $0.30 a month for 5 new tenders a day.

Is this list complete?

No. It shows up to 40 matches per source per week, from national and EU-level portals only. Many regional and state portals are not covered, and some portals publish values in local currency only.
