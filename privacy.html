Privacy

Every Pandapi Tool runs entirely in your web browser. The text and files you paste or drop are processed on your device and are never uploaded, stored or shared. The pages load no ads, no analytics and no third-party scripts, and they set no cookies.

The site is hosted as static files. Like any web host, the host may keep standard server logs (such as IP address and the page requested) for security; we don't use them to track you.

Questions: open an issue on the Pandapi profile at apify.com/blueberry_rainstorm.
