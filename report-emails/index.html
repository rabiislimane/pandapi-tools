Report emails: how they work and how to stop them

Last updated 25 November 2026

The short version. Report emails only go to the email address of your own Apify account, and only if you typed it into your task. To stop them, empty the Email me the report at field in your Apify task and click Save.

Which emails are these?

Two of our APIs can send a short report by email after each run:

  • Competitor price alerts (the Shopify & WooCommerce price monitor): price drops and increases, stock changes, new and removed products.
  • Government tender alerts: new tenders that match your words, with their deadlines.

The email is plain text: a one-line headline, one line per change, and a link to the full results.

Who sends them, and to whom

  • They're sent by Apify's own Send Email tool, inside your Apify account. We don't run an email service and we don't have a mailing list.
  • Apify only lets this tool send to the email address of the account that runs it. So the report can only reach you, the account owner. Nobody can use our APIs to send these emails to someone else.
  • We don't receive or store your email address.
  • By default, you only get an email when something changed. Days with no changes send nothing, unless you chose After every run.
  • Each email is a tiny run in your Apify account and costs well under a cent.

How to stop the emails

  1. Sign in at console.apify.com.
  2. In the left menu, click Saved tasks and open your task (for example "competitor-price-alerts").
  3. Empty the Email me the report at field.
  4. Click Save. The next runs won't send an email.

Every report email ends with these same steps, so you always have them at hand.

How to stop the runs too

The emails come from runs that you scheduled. To stop everything, open Schedules in the Apify left menu and switch your schedule off, or delete it. No runs means no emails and no charges.

Get emails less often, or only when it matters

  • Set Send the email to "only when something changed" (the default).
  • Change your schedule from daily to weekly in Schedules.

Other emails you might get

Apify itself sends account emails, such as invoices, usage warnings and failed-run notices. Those come from Apify, not from us. You can choose which ones you get under Settings, Notifications in Apify Console.

Got an email you didn't expect?

If a report email reached you and you never set one up, someone with access to your Apify account added it. Follow the steps above to stop it, check who has access to your account, and tell us if you need help.
