The free way: the ATS job-board APIs

Most career sites run on an applicant tracking system with a public JSON feed:

https://boards-api.greenhouse.io/v1/boards/airbnb/jobs?content=true
https://api.lever.co/v0/postings/palantir?mode=json
https://api.ashbyhq.com/posting-api/job-board/notion

Free and official. The work: finding which ATS each company uses and its board name, mapping 11 different formats, and remembering last week's jobs so you can see which ones are new or closed.

The API way: a watchlist

  1. Open the Company Jobs Tracker on Apify.
  2. List companies by name, website, careers page or board link. The ATS is detected for you.
  3. Filter by keywords, locations, remote/hybrid, department, seniority or salary.
  4. Turn on trackChanges with a watchlist name and schedule it weekly: you get new jobs (paid) and closed jobs with days open (free).
curl -X POST "https://api.apify.com/v2/acts/blueberry_rainstorm~company-jobs-tracker/run-sync-get-dataset-items?token=$APIFY_TOKEN" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{
  "companies": [
    "Airbnb",
    "stripe.com",
    "https://jobs.lever.co/palantir",
    "https://jobs.ashbyhq.com/notion"
  ],
  "keywords": [
    "engineer",
    "data"
  ],
  "locations": [
    "US",
    "Canada",
    "remote"
  ],
  "trackChanges": true,
  "watchlistName": "eng-north-america"
}'

What you get

Title, department, location, remote/hybrid/onsite, employment type, seniority, salary normalized to min/max/currency/period (from the ATS or parsed from the text), posted date, apply link and description. A free summary row per company counts open roles by department and location, remote share, and new and closed jobs this run.

What it costs

$1 per 1,000 jobs; summaries and closed-job rows are free. A 40-company watchlist with 2,000 open jobs costs $2 the first time, and weekly runs after that charge only new jobs.

Limits

  • Workday career sites are not supported yet (they block cloud servers).
  • LinkedIn and Indeed are not covered: this reads companies' own career sites only.

Questions

Is there a free Greenhouse jobs API?

Yes. Greenhouse's job board API (boards-api.greenhouse.io) is public and free, as are Lever's and Ashby's. The Jobs Tracker reads 11 such systems and adds change tracking for $1 per 1,000 jobs.

How do I track when a company closes a job?

Turn on trackChanges with a watchlist name. Each run compares with the last one and returns closed jobs with their first-seen date, closed date and days open, free of charge.

Which ATS are supported?

Greenhouse, Lever (US and EU), Ashby, SmartRecruiters, Workable, Recruitee, Personio, BambooHR, Rippling, Teamtailor and Breezy.

Do I need to know the company's ATS?

No. Give a name, website or careers page and the ATS and board name are detected.
