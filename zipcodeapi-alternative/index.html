ZipCodeAPI and Zippopotam.us alternatives

Two free services cover most hobby needs: Zippopotam.us for lookups and ZipCodeAPI.com for US distance and radius. Their limits show up quickly in a store locator or a territory map.

Updated 21 November 2026 · by the Pandapi team · We make one of the tools compared here, so we list where the others are better too.

Questions

Is ZipCodeAPI free?

It has a free tier of 10 requests an hour, per its plans page, for US and Canadian codes.

What's a free ZIP code radius API?

ZipCodeAPI's free tier for low volume, or Apify's free $5 monthly credit with Pandapi's API, which covers about 16,000 results.

Does it work outside the US?

Yes, 124 countries from GeoNames data.
