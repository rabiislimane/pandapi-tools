Firecrawl alternatives: free, open-source and pay-per-page options

Firecrawl is the best-known way to turn websites into LLM-ready Markdown. It's also a monthly credit plan, and many people only need a few thousand pages, or a whole docs site once a week. Here are the alternatives, with prices and the cases where Firecrawl is still the right choice.

Updated 21 November 2026 · by the Pandapi team · We make one of the tools compared here, so we list where the others are better too.

Website to Markdown API: $1 per 1,000 pages. Apify's free plan gives you $5 of credit every month, which covers about 5,000 pages a month. No start fee; failed items are free.

The short answer

  • Free and self-hosted: Crawl4AI (open source, Python) or Firecrawl's own open-source version.
  • Free for single pages: Jina Reader (put r.jina.ai/ in front of a URL).
  • Pay per page, no subscription: Pandapi Website to Markdown at $1 per 1,000 pages, failed pages free.
  • Stay with Firecrawl if you need web search, LLM extraction to JSON or browser actions in the same API.

Cost for the same job, 5,000 pages a month: Firecrawl Hobby $19; Pandapi $5 (covered by Apify's free $5 monthly credit). At 100,000 pages Firecrawl Standard ($99) and Pandapi ($100) cost about the same, so pick on features.

Where Firecrawl is better

  • One API for search, scrape, crawl and structured extraction.
  • A large ecosystem: SDKs, LangChain and LlamaIndex loaders, an MCP server.
  • Browser actions (click, scroll, type) before scraping.

Where Pandapi is better

  • No monthly plan: a 300-page site once a month costs $0.30.
  • RAG-ready chunks with heading paths and anchors, and token counts on every page.
  • Failed pages cost nothing.
  • Runs inside Apify: schedules, webhooks, n8n and Make, and an MCP server for agents.

Questions

Is there a free Firecrawl alternative?

Yes. Crawl4AI is open source and free to self-host, Jina Reader converts single URLs free at low rate limits, and Apify's free plan gives $5 of credit a month, which covers about 5,000 pages with Pandapi Website to Markdown.

Is Firecrawl free?

Firecrawl's free plan includes 1,000 page credits a month with 2 concurrent requests. Paid plans start at $19 a month for 5,000 credits, per its pricing page.

Which is cheapest for a one-off crawl?

A pay-per-page API. A 2,000-page docs site costs $2 with Pandapi, with no plan to cancel afterwards.

Can I self-host Firecrawl?

Yes, Firecrawl publishes an open-source version. Self-hosting means running and scaling the browsers yourself.
