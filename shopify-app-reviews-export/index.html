Export Shopify App Store reviews, and spot suspicious ones

Shopify app developers and agencies read competitor reviews to find feature gaps, and many suspect review bursts on rival apps. Shopify has no export button. Here's how to get the reviews into a spreadsheet and check them.

Updated 21 November 2026 · by the Pandapi team

Shopify App Store Reviews Scraper: $0.10 per 1,000 reviews + $0.002 per app. Apify's free plan gives you $5 of credit every month, which covers tens of thousands of reviews a month. No start fee; failed items are free.

The free way

Every app's reviews are public at apps.shopify.com/<app>/reviews, 10 per page, with a newest-first sort and a star filter. For a few pages, copying them by hand works. For hundreds of pages it doesn't, and Shopify's Partner Dashboard shows only your own apps' reviews.

The API way

  1. Open the Shopify App Store Reviews Scraper on Apify.
  2. Paste app links or handles (e.g. judgeme). Several apps per run are fine.
  3. Pick a star range (sent to Shopify, so 1-2 star runs are fast), a since-date and keywords.
  4. Turn on monitoring to get only reviews written since the last run, and schedule it weekly.
curl -X POST "https://api.apify.com/v2/acts/blueberry_rainstorm~shopify-app-store-reviews/run-sync-get-dataset-items?token=$APIFY_TOKEN" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{
  "apps": [
    "https://apps.shopify.com/judgeme",
    "klaviyo-email-marketing"
  ],
  "maxReviewsPerApp": 500,
  "minRating": 1,
  "maxRating": 2,
  "includeSummary": true
}'

Fake-review signals, explained

The free summary row for each app adds a 0-100 suspicion score with plain reasons: review bursts (days with far more reviews than the app's normal rate, and how many of them are 5 stars), 5-star reviews after less than a day of use, repeated wording across reviews, and very short 5-star reviews. A high score is a reason to look closer, not proof. Shopify developers have asked for exactly this in a long community thread about fake reviews.

What you get

Stars, date, text, merchant country, "N months using the app", the developer reply and its date. App details: rating, review count, full star histogram, pricing plans, developer, launch date, categories and the Built for Shopify badge. Merchant store names are left out unless you switch them on.

What it costs

$0.10 per 1,000 reviews and $0.002 per app details row; the summary is free. 5,000 reviews of 3 apps cost about $0.51.

Questions

Can I export reviews of a Shopify app I don't own?

Yes. Shopify App Store reviews are public, so the API can read any listed app.

How are fake reviews detected?

We don't claim to detect them for sure. The summary flags patterns that often come with paid or incentivised reviews: bursts, 5 stars after under a day of use, repeated wording and very short 5-star texts, with a score and reasons.

Does it include store names?

Off by default for privacy. Switch includeStoreNames on if you need them.

Can I monitor a competitor's new reviews?

Yes. Turn on onlyNewReviews and schedule the run; each run returns only reviews written since the last one.
