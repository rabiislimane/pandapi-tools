Billing and refunds

Last updated 25 November 2026

The short version. The tools on this site are free. Our paid APIs are billed by Apify, per result, with no subscription to us. Apify's free plan gives you $5 of credit every month. If a bug on our side charged you for bad results, tell us with the run link and we'll help you get your money back.

The free tools

Everything on this site is free. We never ask for payment details here.

How paid APIs are billed

  • Our APIs run on Apify. Apify takes the payment, sends your invoices and keeps your card details. We never see them.
  • Most of our APIs are pay per result: you pay a small price per item you get (for example per review, per product or per page), plus Apify's small platform cost. The exact price is on each API's Apify page.
  • There is no subscription to Pandapi. You pay only for the runs you start or schedule.
  • Apify's free plan includes $5 of credit every month, with no card needed. Many of our alerts cost well under $1 a month, so they run free.

Keep costs under control

  • Try a small run first, with 1 to 3 items.
  • Set a maximum cost per run in the run options. Apify stops the run when it reaches that amount.
  • Set a monthly usage limit in your Apify billing settings.
  • Scheduled runs keep running until you switch the schedule off. Check your Schedules page if you no longer need them.

Price changes

Under Apify's rules, a price increase can happen at most once a month and only takes effect 14 days after it's announced on the API's page. Price cuts apply right away.

Refunds

Because Apify takes the payment, refunds are made by Apify. Here's what to do:

  1. If a run charged you for results it didn't deliver (empty, broken or clearly wrong because of a bug in our API), open an issue on the API's Issues tab in Apify, with the run link. We'll check the run, fix the bug, and confirm the problem so Apify can refund the charge.
  2. For any other billing question (a plan, an invoice, a payment that failed, a charge you don't recognise), contact Apify support from Apify Console. Apify's terms set the refund rules for your Apify plan.

A run that worked as described but returned fewer results than you hoped (for example a store with only a few products) isn't a bug, so it usually isn't refunded. We're still happy to look at any case: just write to us.

Cancelling

There is nothing to cancel with us. To stop paying, switch off your schedules and stop starting runs. To change or cancel an Apify plan, use the Billing page in Apify Console.

RapidAPI

Some of our APIs are also listed on RapidAPI. If you use them there, RapidAPI's plans, billing and refund rules apply.
