How to pick

  • One file, ruled table: Excel's PDF import or an online converter.
  • Sensitive file: something local: Excel, Tabula or Camelot. Our free PDF to Markdown tool also runs fully in your browser if you need the text.
  • Bank statements or reports every month: an API, so the same job runs on a schedule. See bank statement PDF to Excel.
  • Developer, one known layout: Camelot. See Camelot vs Tabula.

Test any of them with this checklist

  1. Are numbers real numbers (can you SUM the column)?
  2. Is a table that spans two pages one table?
  3. Do wrapped descriptions stay in one cell?
  4. Are negative amounts like (12.00) kept?
  5. Did a borderless table keep its columns?

The Pandapi PDF API was built around that list.

Questions

What is the best free PDF to Excel converter?

For one ruled table, Excel's own PDF import (Microsoft 365) or an online converter. For private files, a local tool like Tabula. For many PDFs or borderless statements, an API; Pandapi's costs $3 per 1,000 PDFs and Apify's free credit covers about 1,600 a month.

Can I convert PDF to Excel without uploading?

Yes: Excel's PDF import, Tabula and Camelot run on your computer.

Why do my numbers come out as text?

Thousands separators, currency signs and brackets for negatives make Excel treat cells as text. A converter that parses numbers fixes this; otherwise use Data › Text to Columns.
