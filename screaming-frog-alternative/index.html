Keep Screaming Frog when

You do technical SEO audits. It checks far more than links, and nothing here replaces it.

Use a cloud checker when

You want broken links found every week without opening a desktop app, results in Slack or a sheet, and to pay only for what you check: a 3,000-link site costs $2.40 a run.

Questions

Is Screaming Frog free?

The free version crawls up to 500 URLs per crawl and can't save crawls. A licence is £199 per user per year, per its pricing page.

What's a free alternative for broken links?

Google Search Console's Not found report, Screaming Frog's free version for small sites, or a cloud checker with free credit: Apify's $5 a month covers about 6,000 links with Pandapi.

Can it run every week automatically?

Yes. Schedule the run on Apify and send the results by email, Slack or webhook.
