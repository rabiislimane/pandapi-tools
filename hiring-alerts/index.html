Hiring signals: get an alert when your target companies post new jobs

Name the companies you sell to, recruit for or want to work at. Each morning you get only the jobs they posted since yesterday: title, department, location, remote or hybrid and salary where published, live from their own career sites. A company hiring 5 salespeople or its first data engineer is a buying signal.

About $0.13 a month to watch 20 companies posting 30 new jobs a week (the first run, which lists every open job, costs about $1 per 1,000 jobs). Apify's free plan gives you $5 of credit every month.

What the daily alert tells you

  • New jobs each target company posted since the last run, with a link to apply or read more.
  • Title, department, location, remote/hybrid/onsite, salary range where the company publishes it.
  • Closed jobs (free): roles that were filled or pulled.
  • A free hiring summary per company: open jobs, posted in the last 30 days, top departments, remote share.
  • Filters: keywords ("sales", "data engineer"), locations, departments, remote only, only jobs with a salary.

Set it up in 3 minutes, no code

  1. Open the ready-made task. Click Set it up in 3 minutes, create a free Apify account (no card), replace the example companies with yours (a name or their careers page link, one per line), and click Save & Start. The first run lists every open job as the starting point.
  2. Make it run every morning. In Apify's left menu click Schedules, then Create new. Choose Daily, click Add > Task and pick your task, then Save and Enable. Apify's guide: Schedules.
  3. Get it in Slack or Google Drive. On the task, open Integrations and add Slack or Google Drive, so new jobs land where your team works.

Questions

Which career sites work?

Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, SmartRecruiters, Workable, Recruitee, BambooHR, Rippling, Teamtailor, Personio and Breezy. Type a company name or paste its careers page. Workday sites aren't supported yet.

How much does it cost?

$1 per 1,000 jobs returned; closed jobs and summaries are free. The first run lists every open job, then 20 companies posting 30 new jobs a week cost about $0.13 a month.

Why is hiring a sales signal?

A company hiring for a team usually gets budget for that team's tools. New sales, data or security roles are a common trigger for outreach.

Can I watch only some roles?

Yes. Add keywords, locations or departments and only matching new jobs are returned.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. You open the ready-made task, type company names, click Schedule and add Slack or Google Drive.
