Stay with PurgoMalum when

Your users write in English, volume is small and a missed word is not a problem.

Switch when

You moderate reviews, chat or usernames in several languages, or users are actively getting around the filter.

The instant API has PurgoMalum-compatible endpoints, so it's a base-URL change plus your Apify token.
