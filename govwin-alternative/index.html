GovWin alternative: daily tender alerts on your keywords for small bid teams

Deltek GovWin IQ is the heavyweight of government market intelligence: analyst research, forecasts, contract history and buyer contacts across US federal, state and local and Canadian markets, sold as an annual subscription on quote. Many small bid teams use only one part of it: a morning list of new tenders that match their keywords. That part costs cents.

Set it up in 3 minutes, no code

  1. Open the ready-made task and create a free Apify account (Google sign-in works).
  2. Type your keywords (and words to exclude, countries or NAICS/CPV codes if you like) and your email in Email me the report at. Click Save & Start.
  3. Click Schedules, then Create new, then Daily, and add your task. Every morning you get only the tenders you haven't seen.

Questions

Is there a cheaper alternative to GovWin IQ?

For daily alerts on new tenders, yes: a scheduled alert covering SAM.gov, TED, the UK, CanadaBuys and the World Bank costs about $0.60 a month for about 10 new tenders a day, inside Apify's free $5 monthly credit. For forecasts, state and local coverage and contract history, GovWin IQ or GovSpend are still the tools to compare.

How much does GovWin IQ cost?

Deltek doesn't publish prices. It sells annual subscriptions quoted by market (federal, state and local, Canada), number of users and modules. Third-party estimates start around $5,000 a year.

Can I get government tender alerts for free?

Yes. SAM.gov, TED and CanadaBuys each have free saved-search emails for their own portal. The alert here puts all of them in one email for about $0.60 a month, which Apify's free credit covers.

Does it cover US state and local bids?

No. In the US it covers federal notices from SAM.gov.
