TheirStack alternatives for tracking company hiring

Job-data providers answer "which companies are hiring for X?" across the whole market. Many sales and recruiting teams ask a narrower question: "what are these 50 companies hiring for, and what changed this week?" That one is cheaper to answer.

Choose TheirStack when

You're searching the market (every company using Snowflake and hiring data engineers), or you need tech-stack data.

Choose a watchlist when

You know the companies: your accounts, competitors or portfolio. Live career-site data is exact, and closed jobs plus days open tell you more than a snapshot.

Questions

Is there a free alternative to TheirStack?

For known companies, the ATS job-board APIs (Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby) are free to call yourself. The Jobs Tracker reads 11 of them for $1 per 1,000 jobs, and Apify's free credit covers about 5,000 jobs a month.

How much does TheirStack cost?

Per its docs, there's a free plan with 200 API credits a month for new users, and paid plans; a job in the job search endpoint costs 1 credit.

Can I see when a job was closed?

Yes, with trackChanges on: closed jobs come with first-seen date, closed date and days open, free.
